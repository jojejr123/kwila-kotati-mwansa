About

Precise, curious, dependable

A biomedical scientist who turns bench results into data that shapes antimicrobial resistance policy.

I trained at Hassan II University of Casablanca, earning a degree in Biomedical Technologies in 2018. I then rotated through nine departments at the University Teaching Hospital and Levy Mwanawasa University Teaching Hospital in Lusaka.

Since 2020 I have led microbiology at Livingstone University Teaching Hospital. I am the Ministry of Health's provincial microbiology focal point and sit on the monitoring and evaluation team for Fleming Fund-supported surveillance sites. I am now studying for a Master of Public Health.
